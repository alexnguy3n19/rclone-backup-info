Personal rclone backup client

This is a private, single-user configuration of the open-source rclone utility. It copies and verifies owner-selected backups and archives in the owner's Google Drive. This is not a public file-sharing service or an official Google product.

This informational website does not sign users in or accept credentials. The backup client runs on the owner's computers and server.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

The configured client requests Google Drive permission to read, create, update and delete files as needed for owner-directed backup and recovery operations. Individual backup tasks restrict their operations to configured paths; this restriction is in the task configuration, not a narrower Google permission.

OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted configuration files on owner-controlled machines and are transmitted to Google for authentication. File contents and metadata are processed by those machines and Google Drive. Local recovery copies and operational logs may also be retained by the owner.

Google Drive data is used only for these personal backup and archive operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, published by this website, or supplied to an AI service by this client.

Retention and deletion are controlled by the owner. Removing a local working copy does not automatically remove a retained backup. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections; revocation does not delete existing backups.

This page has no forms, analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Google processes data under its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

The operator can be contacted through the user-support email displayed on the Google consent screen. This client is for the operator's own account, not third-party customers.
